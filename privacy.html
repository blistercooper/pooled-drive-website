Pooled Drive: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Pooled Drive is a personal app that runs as an Android app and as a website. This page explains what information it uses and what happens to it. Back to the home page.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with a Google account, you give the app permission to see your email address and to create and manage the files it creates in your Google Drive (the Google Drive “drive.file” permission). The app can also read the amount of storage used in the account. If you turn on phone backup, it reads the photos and videos on your phone that you choose to back up.

How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the features you see in the app: showing your storage, uploading and downloading files, playing them, backing them up, copying or moving them between your accounts, and sharing links.

Where the information goes

Pooled Drive has no server and no developer account that receives your data. Your files and account details travel only between your phone and Google. The app does not sell or share your information with anyone, and it contains no advertising or analytics.

What is stored on your phone

The Android app stores, on your phone only: an encrypted sign-in key for each account you add, and a list of the files it has stored (names, sizes, and which account holds each one).

The website version

The website at this address talks to Google Drive directly from your browser, with the same permission as the app. It has no server and no database. Your browser keeps your list of account email addresses, folder identifiers and settings in its local storage, and keeps Google's short-lived access tokens in session storage, which your browser deletes when you close the tab. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. Files you upload, download, preview or share go only between your browser and Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Pooled Drive’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can remove an account in the app or on the website at any time, which deletes its sign-in key from your phone or its details from your browser. You can also revoke the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already stored in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them.
