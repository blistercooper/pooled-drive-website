Pooled Drive
FeaturesPrivacy

One cloud, made from your own Google accounts.

Pooled Drive adds up the free space of the Google accounts you own and shows it as a single cloud. Upload a file and it chooses the best account for it. Use it in your browser or on your Android phone.

Open the web app Get the Android app

What it does for you

One pool of storage

Sign in with several Google accounts you own and see all their free space together.

Smart placement

Big files go where there is the most room. Small files fill the gaps. You never choose by hand.

Mail-safe limit

Pick how full each account may get, from 50 to 100 percent, so mail keeps arriving.

Morning backup

The Android app backs up new photos and videos every day. Only new or changed files are sent.

Share links

Share a file the way you would in Google Drive, to anyone with the link or to chosen people.

View and play

Open photos, play videos and music, and read PDFs, with all your files in one list.

How it works

Sign in with Google

You sign in on Google's own page. The app never sees your password.

Add your accounts

Add as many of your own accounts as you like. Each one adds its free space to the pool.

Upload and relax

Files go straight from your device to Google Drive. There is no Pooled Drive server in between.

Good to know

Pooled Drive only works with files it creates itself, in a folder called Pooled Drive in each account. It cannot see the rest of your Drive. A single file has to fit inside one account. Google's rules discourage using several accounts to get around storage limits, and Google can restrict accounts, so use the pool for files you can replace and keep a second copy of anything important.
