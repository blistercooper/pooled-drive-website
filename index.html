Pooled Drive

A personal Android app for using several of your own Google Drive accounts as one.

What it does

Pooled Drive lets you sign in with one or more Google accounts that you own and see their combined free storage in one place. When you upload a file, the app chooses which of your accounts to store it in. You can also back up photos and videos from your phone, browse and play your files, copy or move them between your accounts, and share a link to a file the same way you would in Google Drive.

How it works

  • You sign in through Google’s own sign-in page. The app never sees your password.
  • The app only works with files it creates itself, in a folder called “Pooled Drive” in each account.
  • There is no Pooled Drive server. Your files go directly between your phone and Google Drive.
